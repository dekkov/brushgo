# Brushy

Brushy helps people build a twice-daily toothbrushing habit with selfie check-ins.

## Language

**Brushy**:
The product people use to record their brushing habit and look back on their check-ins.
_Avoid_: Brushgo

**Friend**:
Another Brushy user with whom a person has mutually accepted an active friendship. A person can have at most five friends.
_Avoid_: Follower

**Friend request**:
A proposal to become friends requiring the recipient's acceptance.

**Friend invite**:
A reusable, resettable link or code that lets another person send its owner a friend request. Possessing an invite does not grant friendship or selfie access.

**Audience**:
The visibility choice fixed when a check-in is saved: only its owner, all friends, or selected friends.

**All friends**:
All currently accepted friends, including people who become friends after a selfie was shared.

**Selected friends**:
The individual active friendships chosen for a check-in. Ending and renewing a friendship does not restore its former selected-only access.

**Feed**:
A combined view of a person's own selfies and friends' selfies shared with them, showing the owner's name and current brushing streak.

**Display name**:
The editable name shown on a person's requests, profile, and selfies. It need not be unique and is not their email address.

**Replacement selfie**:
A new selfie superseding the existing selfie for the same morning or evening check-in, with a new feed position and its own audience without additional streak credit.

**Blocked person**:
A person prevented from requesting friendship or accessing the blocker's shared photos. Blocking ends friendship; unblocking does not restore it.
